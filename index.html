A few useful details

Before you arrive.

How can I check room availability?

Enter your dates and guest count in the enquiry bar. You can then continue to the third-party booking listing or send an enquiry to the hotel team. Live availability is not yet connected directly to this website.

Where is Aurora Grove located?

Aurora Grove is listed on Manu Temple Road in Old Manali, Himachal Pradesh, postal code 175131.

What room types are listed?

Public accommodation listings describe a Double Room and a Four-Bed Mixed Dormitory Room. Confirm the exact room type and inclusions before booking.

Can I enquire about special requests?

Yes. Use the enquiry action to prepare a message with your dates, guest count and requests. The hotel's direct WhatsApp number or email needs to be added in the site configuration before direct messaging can work.
